AgriLearn JSS - Site Plan

Kelechi Jesil Samuel | Final Project | WDD 131

1. Site Name

AgriLearn JSS

I chose this name because it clearly shows the purpose: Learning Agriculture for Junior Secondary School. It's short, easy to remember, and direct for students.

2. Site Purpose

AgriLearn JSS helps Junior Secondary students (JSS1-JSS3) learn Agricultural Science easily with simple notes, crop diagrams, and quizzes. The site will provide:

  • Simplified lesson notes for JSS Agricultural Science
  • Diagrams of crops, farm tools, and livestock
  • Interactive quizzes for revision
  • Download link to mobile app for offline learning

3. Target Audience

Primary: JSS1-JSS3 students in Nigeria (Age 10-14) who find Agricultural Science difficult.

Secondary: Teachers and parents who want simple materials to teach agriculture.

Scenarios:

  • "I need simple notes on types of crops for my JSS1 exam."
  • "I want diagrams of farm tools with their names."
  • "I want to test myself with a quiz before my CA test."

Modern, friendly, and easy for young students to read. Used for HERO and feature titles.

Body: Open Sans Regular

Very readable on phone screens. Perfect for lesson notes and paragraphs.
