Taste of Lagos - Site Plan

Kelechi Jesil Samuel - WDD131 Project

1. Site Name: Taste of Lagos

Name: Taste of Lagos

Logo: TL in circle above

Reason: I chose Taste of Lagos because it immediately tells visitors what the site is about - authentic Nigerian and Lagos cuisine. It is memorable, relevant to my location in Lagos, and good for branding a food/restaurant guide site.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Taste of Lagos is to be the #1 online guide for anyone who wants to cook or eat authentic Lagos food. The site will provide:

  • Step-by-step Nigerian recipes (Jollof Rice, Egusi, Suya)
  • List of best local restaurants and markets in Lagos
  • Cooking tips and video tutorials
  • User reviews and ability to submit own recipes

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1: "I am a student who just moved to Lagos. What ingredients do I need to make Jollof Rice and where can I buy them cheap in Yaba market?"

Scenario 2: "I am a tourist visiting Ikeja. Which restaurants near me serve authentic Egusi Soup and what are their prices and opening hours?"
