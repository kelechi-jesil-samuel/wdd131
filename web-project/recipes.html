Lagos Party Jollof

Ingredients: 4 cups rice, 5 tomatoes, 3 tatashe, 2 onions, curry, thyme, 1L chicken stock.
Steps: 1. Blend pepper 2. Fry in oil 3. Add rice & stock 4. Cover with foil for smoky taste.
